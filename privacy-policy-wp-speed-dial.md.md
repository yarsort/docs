# Privacy Policy - WP Speed Dial

**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

WP Speed Dial is a browser extension that replaces the default new tab page with a customizable dashboard for quick access to favorite websites.

We respect your privacy and designed WP Speed Dial to work without user tracking, analytics, or unnecessary data collection.

## 2. Information Processed

WP Speed Dial may process the following information to provide its functionality:

- Website URLs and titles added to shortcut tiles.
- User-created groups and layout preferences.
- Extension settings and appearance preferences.
- Website screenshots selected or generated for shortcut previews.
- Information about browser tabs when required for user-requested features.

## 3. Local Data Storage

WP Speed Dial stores shortcut information, groups, preferences, and saved previews locally in the user's browser.

This information is not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information is processed exclusively to provide the extension's functionality, including managing website shortcuts, displaying customized new tab pages, and generating visual previews.

WP Speed Dial does not use this information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or tracking browsing behavior.

## 5. Data Sharing

WP Speed Dial does not sell, share, or transfer user data to the developer or third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

## 6. Browser Permissions

WP Speed Dial uses browser permissions to support its core functionality.

- **Tabs:** Used for browser tab interactions required for website shortcuts and previews.
- **Optional website access:** May be requested when needed for user-initiated website preview generation.

Optional permissions are requested only when necessary for the relevant functionality.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains in the browser until it is removed by the user or cleared through browser data management.

Users can manage or delete their shortcuts and preferences within the extension where supported.

## 8. Third-Party Services

WP Speed Dial does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

When users open their saved websites, those websites operate independently and are subject to their own privacy policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

WP Speed Dial is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data handling practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page.

## 11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the WP Speed Dial extension store listing.

## 12. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

WP Speed Dial's use of information obtained through browser permissions complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information is used solely to provide or improve the extension's disclosed functionality. It is not used for personalized advertising, sold to third parties, or accessed by humans except where permitted by applicable policies.
